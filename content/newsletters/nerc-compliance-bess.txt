Grid Reliability & Market Outlook
NERC Highlights Elevated Risk in MISO Region
NERC’s latest sensitivity analysis for the 2025 Long-Term Reliability Assessment (LTRA) highlights a cautiously optimistic outlook:
~25 GW of new generation (via MISO ERAS) included in analysis 
Risk remains “elevated” through 2027, improving to “normal” by 2030 
Key risks persist due to: 
Supply chain constraints 
Transmission interconnection delays 
Natural gas infrastructure limitations 
Regulatory and permitting challenges 
Key Insight:
﻿
Even with significant generation additions, timely execution remains the biggest risk driver for grid reliability.

NERC Q2 2026 Standards Quarterly Outlook
NERC’s latest quarterly update emphasizes several critical developments:
Ongoing revisions aligned with FERC Order No. 909 
New initiatives addressing large load reliability risks (e.g., data centers) 
Upcoming compliance requirements for: 
Category 2 Generator Owners (GO) 
Generator Operators (GOP) 


What This Means:

Asset owners must proactively prepare for evolving compliance frameworks and increased scrutiny on inverter-based resources and large load impacts.

Technology Deep Dive
LFP Battery Evolution: Moving Beyond 314Ah to 587Ah+
The BESS industry is undergoing a major shift in battery design:
Key Trends
LFP market share: 48% → 85% (2021–2024) 
Cost reduction: ~93% since 2010 
LFP now ~40% cheaper than NMC 
Next-Generation Cells
Manufacturers (CATL, BYD, CALB, EVE, Hithium) are moving toward:
587Ah and larger cells 
Up to 12,000+ cycle life 
20+ year lifespan 
~95–96% round-trip efficiency 
Why 587Ah is the “Sweet Spot”
Higher energy density per container 
Reduced component complexity 
Improved thermal management (fewer cells) 
Maintains transport limits (~45-ton container threshold) 
Engineering Implications
Simplified system design 
Enhanced reliability and lifecycle performance 
Increased importance of thermal management and BMS optimization 
Looking Ahead:

While LFP dominates today, emerging technologies like LMFP and sodium-ion batteries may influence future BESS architectures.
