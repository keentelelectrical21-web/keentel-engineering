Global Trends in Electrical Power Engineering
Grid Resilience is Now a Global Priority
Utilities across the U.S., Europe, and Asia are intensifying investment in grid modernization to enhance resilience against climate-related events, cyber threats, and rising loads from electric vehicles (EVs) and data centers.
Surge in Renewable Energy Integration
Global renewable capacity grew by 15% year-over-year in Q1 2025, led by solar and wind energy. With aggressive net-zero targets, the demand for grid interconnection studies and inverter-based resource (IBR) modeling is surging.
Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) Go Mainstream
BESS is now standard infrastructure. ISOs like CAISO and ERCOT mandate comprehensive modeling, protection coordination, and dynamic simulation studies for all new BESS projects.

Regulatory & Compliance Highlights
IEEE 2800™-2022 Compliance in Focus
Generator Owners (GOs) must now ensure their IBR facilities meet IEEE 2800 standards for grid-forming controls, fault ride-through, and voltage/frequency regulation.
NERC PRC-006-5 Implementation
Keentel supports clients with Protection System Maintenance Programs (PSMPs) and transformer thermal assessments aligned with TPL-007 GMD impact assessments.
MOD-032 Dynamic Model Accuracy
Model validation and dynamic data compliance are now essential. Keentel Engineering ensures model accuracy for PJM, MISO, and CAISO interconnection queue submissions.

Keentel in Action: Recent Engineering Projects
Completed a 345 kV [Interconnection Study](https://keentelengineering.com/optimizing-grid-interconnection-compliance-with-pjm-dynamic-modeling-standards) for a hybrid solar + BESS facility in Texas
PJM-compliant EMT model validation using PSCAD successfully delivered
Partnering with EDF Renewables on behind-the-meter NERC studies for the Soluna Project
Engineered HV substation grounding systems per IEEE Std 80-2000 for a California utility client

Tech Spotlight: Advancing Electrical Engineering
Digital Substations: Adoption of IEC 61850 is accelerating, offering reduced copper cabling and improved protection system communication.
AI for Predictive Maintenance: Advanced analytics and machine learning are transforming transformer diagnostics, enabling outage forecasting.
Power Quality Monitoring: With increasing DERs and non-linear loads, utilities are investing in real-time power quality (PQ) monitoring and harmonic analysis.
